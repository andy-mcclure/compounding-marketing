## The Core Offer: Digital Employees You Own

A fully configured and trained digital employee, living inside cloud accounts the client owns, that the client talks to through whatever they already use — Slack, email, or a web chat front end. Each one does a specific, revenue-generating job. It is not tied to one AI model company; the client's accounts can run whichever models do the job best.

Built inside the client's accounts, on the client's infrastructure, producing intelligence that belongs to the client permanently. Longview Marketing is the architect and builder. The client is the owner. When the engagement evolves or ends, the system stays. Fire us, and your team stays.

**Term and guarantee.** A one-year subscription. Longview configures and trains the employee until it operates the way the client wants it to — or the client's money back. The guarantee carries the risk, not the term.

**Price.** Stated publicly only as a comparison: a digital employee costs per year roughly what a human employee doing the same job costs per month. The number is worked out on a call against the client's own payroll. No dollar figures are published.

**Roles Longview runs for itself** (name these by role, not by agent name, in public): SEO manager, LinkedIn content, Google Ads, cold email, lead research, executive assistant. Andy is the primary subject — every role is tested on Longview before it's offered to a client.

**What this is:** Permanent capability installed inside the client's business — a system that compounds results whether Longview Marketing is involved or not.

**What this is not:** A monthly deliverables package. A campaign management retainer. Access to agency tools and infrastructure. "We handle your marketing."

**What the client should expect** (in Andy's words): there's no real shortcut to training an employee, whether they are human or digital. The first few weeks take daily review and feedback from the owner. That is the work the subscription pays Longview to lead.

## The Marketing Ownership Audit

**The entry point diagnostic.**

Reveals where a business's marketing intelligence currently lives and who controls it. Reviews accounts, platforms, AI tool usage, vendor relationships, data ownership, and content workflows to determine the degree of ownership the business actually has.

**Produces:** A scored assessment identifying gaps between current state and what is possible under a Compounding Marketing model.

**Standalone deliverable** — owned by the client regardless of what happens next. A roadmap for closing the gaps is a separate paid engagement.

## The Winter Readiness Assessment

**Painting contractor entry point.**

Surfaces gaps in the marketing system relative to what it would take to fill a winter calendar with interior painting work — before winter arrives.

**Urgency driver:** Calendar. The window to build the pipeline closes when exterior season ends.

**Diagnosis only.** Action plan is a separate paid engagement.

## Engagement Model Principles

- One-year agreements for digital employees, with the guarantee carrying the risk — not the term
- No pre-built packages
- Customized engagements built around the client's specific business need
- Longview manages the process — client contributes direction and feedback, not coordination
- Client time investment: daily review for the first few weeks of training; two to five hours per month once the employee is running
- Operational standard: *How is it going? What's next? Make it so.*

## The Ownership Commitment

At any point, the client can export everything built about their business — context files, strategic intelligence, frameworks. Portable by design. This isn't a marketing promise. It's a structural commitment baked into how the work is done.

If the client ends the relationship, the digital employee and everything it has learned stay with the client and keep working without Longview. Fire us, and your team stays.
